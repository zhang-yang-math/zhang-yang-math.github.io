Publications

Complete Calabi-Yau metrics on noncompact abelian fibered threefolds

With Ruiming Liang. arXiv:2501.15205

Abstract

In this article, we construct new families of complete Calabi-Yau metrics on abelian fibrations X over ℂ, which generalizes the type ALG, ALG*, ALH, ALH* gravitational instantons to higher dimensions. We also provide a projective compactification for X so that the compactified variety has negative canonical divisor.

Demazure's structure theorem for the automorphism group of a toric variety: old and new

With Chenzi Jin and Yanir A. Rubinstein. arXiv:2511.14122

Abstract

This article provides an accessible and simplified proof of Demazure's 1970 structure theorem for the automorphism group of a compact smooth toric variety, previously considered quite inaccessible. Moreover, we simplify Cox's extension of Demazure's theorem to orbifolds, obtained over the years 1995–2014, allowing us to generalize it to any compact (arbitrarily singular) toric variety and toric log pairs. Finally, we further generalize the theorem to non-compact toric varieties obtained as the complement of toric subvariety of a codimension at least 2, resolving a problem originating in Oda's classic 1988 book.

Symmetry notions for toric Fanos

With Chenzi Jin and Yanir A. Rubinstein. arXiv:2511.14122

Abstract

This article explores and (mostly) surveys various notions of symmetry for toric varieties. These notions range from algebraic geometric, complex geometric, representation theoretic, combinatorial, convex geometric, to geometric stability. The main theorem gives the relationship between these notions. Further, examples complement this result by showing these relations are essentially optimal.

Quantization commutes with reduction of Chern-Simons gauge theory

With Geyang Dai and Ruiming Liang. arXiv:2601.09666

Abstract

We prove an infinite-dimensional version of “quantization commutes with reduction” in the framework of geometric quantization of Chern-Simons gauge theory, focusing on the genus one case. The proof is complex-analytic and relies on the Atiyah-Bott stack and the Chern-Simons line bundle.

Remarks on the complex structures on ℙ3 and S2 × S4

With Ruiming Liang and Chenhan Liu. arXiv:2608.29651

Abstract

Assuming the validity of the recently proposed “A compact complex threefold fibred by tori over the projective line, and the six-sphere”, we construct an exotic complex structure on ℙ3, distinct from the point-blowup structures of Huckleberry, Kebekus, and Peternell. Then we perform an Atiyah flop to produce a complex structure on the standard smooth manifold S2 × S4.
